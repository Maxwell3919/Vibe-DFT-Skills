nc

INTEGER

Default:

1

nc(1), nc(2), and nc(3) indicate the size of the
supercell for the visualization of the squared modulus of
the Hubbard projector functions.
Note, this supercell must match the k-points grid size
otherwise the resulting WFs are wrong.
